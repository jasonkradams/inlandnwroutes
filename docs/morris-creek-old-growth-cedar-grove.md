# Morris Creek Old Growth Cedar Grove

## Morris creek old growth cedar grove trail 749

## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district
info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed. Altho this is a short
hike, it is filled with 500 year old cedars. At only 80 acres, its walk is .75 miles. Take the time on your
way to the Giant Western Red Cedars, to visit this grove. In the spring and summer, take mosquito head nets,
and cover up.

## Directions

From the Elk Creek Falls National Recreation Trail, drive north past Elk River on Hwy 382 to F.R. #1969.

## Cool things close by

Elk Creek Falls National Recreation Area, the a giant Western Red Cedars, Dworshak Reservoir, and the Hobo
Cedar Grove.

## Hazards

Mosquitos.
