# Lunch Peak  Mount Pend Orielle

*Lunch peak 6412’ & Mount pend oreille 6755’ ​trail #67*

## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
​This 8 mile RT hike start up at 6412’ at the Lunch Peak rental lookout tower. From the lookout, the Trail #67 heads north for 4 miles along an undulating ridge to a high point of 6755 foot Mount Pend Oreille

Do not go left up the hill. Follow the trail sign.
Both Lunch Peak and Mount Pend Oreille offer incredible views.
To the south east is the Proposed Scotchman Peaks Wilderness, at 16 miles. And to the ESE 26 miles is Snowshoe Peak in the Cabinet Mountain Wilderness. 
To the SW 20+- miles is the views look down Lake Pend Oreille.
​To the west about 32 miles is the American Selkirks

You will see from the images below, that this day hike is spectacular to say the least.
And a pretty easy walking.

## Directions

From Sandpoint, drive east on Hwy 200 for about 12 miles to the Trestle Creek Road #275. Turn left (north east) for about 12 miles, and turn left at a switchback onto F.R #1091 for 4 miles to the Lunch Peak Lookout.

​Trestle Creek road is in very good condition.

## Cool things close by

Lake Darling, Lake Estelle, and Moose Lake & Mountain.

## Hazards

The only hazard on this hike, is wandering off the trail as you gawk at the intense beauty

## R & p

Mr. Sub, Eichardt’s, Jalapeños and the Burger Express.
