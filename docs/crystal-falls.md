# Crystal Falls

## Crystal falls, washington

---

WATERFALL : CRYSTAL FALLS, Stevens County
STREAM OR RIVER:  Little Pend Orielle River
DROP: About 60 feet in two drops
WATERFALL TYPE: tiered
​DISTANCE CAR TO FALLS: about 50 feet off the Colville-Tiger road, (also known as Hwy 20)
MAPS: Colville National Forest, Park Rapids topo
Gps: 48°51"21" n 117°65’69” w

---

## Description

Crystal Falls is one of the first falls I visited in my youth. It's only 50 feet off HWY 20. Look for the Park Rapids sign and parking area.
Crystal Falls drops about 60' in two tiers. There are user built "trails" to get down to the pool level. In spring, while the run off is heavy,
I've photographed it from pool level. The photo looks like a small dog sitting. I'll add that image when I find it.
Any time you are in the area, be sure to stop and enjoy Crystal Falls

---

## Directions

From Spokane head north up Hwy 395 to Colville, and east on Hwy 20 for 14.7 miles to the Park Rapids Sign and parking area.
From Newport, head north west, staying on Hwy 20 past the Flodelle Campground, for about 3.5 mile to Park Rapids.

---

## Cool things close by

Douglas Falls Grange Falls, Meyers Falls and Sweet Water Falls at the Sweet Creek Rest Area on Hwy 31 north of Ione.

## Hazards

All waterfalls are a hazard, due to their slippery nature. always be extra careful near any waterfall.
