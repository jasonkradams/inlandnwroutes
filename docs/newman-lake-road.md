# Newman Lake Road

## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
Newman Lake is a semi long lake with 6 bays to explore.
Disregard the n honey suckle launch mark on map above.

## Attractions

McKenzie Conservation Area

## Directions

Drive east on Hwy 290 which is E. Trent Road, to Moab, Wa.
Turn left (N) onto N. Star Road and drive tons "T" with E Hauser Lake Road for less then .5 miles to the Newman Lake Public Fishing area.

## Cool things close by

Hauser Lake, Twin Lakes, and Spirit Lake
