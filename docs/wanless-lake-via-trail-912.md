# Wanless Lake via Swamp Creek (Trail #912 & #912A)

## Description

Swamp Creek Trail #912 follows the scenic Swamp Creek drainage deep into the Cabinet Mountain Wilderness,
ascending to Lost Buck Pass. From Lost Buck Pass, Lake Creek Trail #656 descends to Upper and Lower Geiger
Lakes, while South Divide Trail #360A traverses a major ridgeline out to the southern boundary of the C.M.W.

Buck Lake Trail #912A branches off for 0.38 miles to Buck Lake, offering primitive shoreline campsites and
native cutthroat trout fishing.

## Trail Statistics

- **Swamp Creek Trail #912:** 12.13 miles one way (Trailhead elevation 2,686', Lost Buck Pass 5,907').
- **Buck Lake Trail #912A:** 0.38 miles one way (Buck Lake elevation 4,389').
- **Net Elevation Gain:** 1,703' to Buck Lake; 3,221' to Lost Buck Pass.

## Directions

From Highway 200 near Trout Creek, turn north onto FSR #1129 (Swamp Creek Road) and drive approximately 6
miles to the Swamp Creek Trailhead.
