# Coeur d'Alene River Trail 20

## Description

The Coeur d'Alene River National Recreation Trail (#20) is one of the flagship river canyon routes in the
Idaho Panhandle National Forests. Extending 15.3 miles one-way along the upper North Fork Coeur d'Alene
River from Forest Road 208 near Spion Kop Rock to Martin Creek and Magee, the trail traverses rolling bluffs
and lush forest benches above the water.

Although the net elevation difference between the lower and upper trailheads is only 527 feet, the trail's
frequent short climbs and dips accumulate nearly 2,000 feet of total elevation gain over its full length.
The river corridor is famous for catch-and-release fly fishing for native Westslope Cutthroat Trout, deep
swimming holes, and remote wilderness character.

## Access & Directions

- **Lower (South) Trailhead**: From Coeur d'Alene, drive east on I-90 to Exit 43 (Kingston). Drive north on
  Forest Highway 9 for 23 miles to Forest Road 208. Follow FR-208 north for 25 miles to the lower
  trailhead near Spion Kop Rock.
- **Upper (North) Trailhead**: From Wallace, drive north for 16.3 miles over Dobson Pass on FR-456 to FH-9.
  Turn east for 1.8 miles on FH-9 to FR-208. Follow FR-208 north for 1.6 miles to Shoshone Creek FR-412,
  then drive north on FR-412 for 22 miles to the upper trailhead near Martin Creek.
