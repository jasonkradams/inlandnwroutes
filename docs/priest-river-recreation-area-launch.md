## Description

This off the P.O. River launch is at the confluence of the Priest River and the P.O. Rivers.
Within the Priest River Recreation Area is the Priest River Park, with plenty of parking, restrooms, and a swimming area.

## Attractions

Off the P.O. River at the confluence of the Priest River.

## Directions

From the stop light in Priest River, drive east on Hwy 2 for 1.3 miles to the entrance, next to the saw mill.

## Cool things close by

The American Selkirks, Upper & Lower Priest Lakes, Round Lake State Park, and the Pend Orielle River.
