# Medical Lake Launch

## Description

We have added the area's sheriff emergency phone numbers for each trip write-up under the ranger district info. If an emergency occurs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.

Medical Lake and West Medical Lake launch options provide access to quiet waters west of Spokane.

## Directions

Drive west on I-90 from Spokane to Highway 902 exit toward Medical Lake. Follow Highway 902 into Medical Lake to access the waterfront launch areas.
