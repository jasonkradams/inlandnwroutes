# Hall Mountain 6233 Trail 588

## Description

The start of the trail soon joins an old roadbed for 100 yards before it leaves the road and climbs
steadily, before dropping into Noisy Creek where it crosses the creek. From here it follows Noisy Creek
for 1.5 miles where it switchbacks to the north, where it meets up with trail #s 533 & 540. At the junction with
533, 540 and 588, turn left (west) onto trail 540 to the summit (6,233'). This trail is up, so plan ahead.

## Directions

From Newport, drive up SH #31 to a half a mile before Ione, and turn right (east) onto SH #9345, the
Sullivan Lake Road. Drive 13 miles to the south end of Sullivan Lake and the Noisy Creek campground.

Special note: near Ione is the Sweetwater Rest Area. Of course stop to pee, but walk the trail to the
waterfalls behind the restrooms.

## Hazards

Steep and long trail.

## Cool Things Close By

Sweet Water Rest Area and Falls, Sullivan Lake, Elk Creek Falls, the Salmo-Priest Wilderness, Crowell Ridge,
Metaline Falls, the Pend Oreille River, Boundary Dam, and Crawford State Park & Gardner Cave.
