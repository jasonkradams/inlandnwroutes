## Description

Windy Bay is one of the larger bays on Lake CDA, and as the name implies, it can be very windy.
Up the SE end of the lake is Lake Creek to paddle up if its windy on Windy Bay.

## Attractions

Huge Bay with a nice creek to paddle up.

## Directions

Drive south on Hwy 95 from CDA to the W. Settlers Road, and turn right. Drive a short distance to W. Bitter Road, and turn left, and drive back under the Hwy , staying on W. Bitter Road, to S. Lakewood Cove Road, and turn left almost to the lake, but turn left onto W. Ice Storm Road, to the 4th dock from the end

## Cool things close by

Sun Up Bay, Rockford Bay, Rockford Point, and the main body of Lake CDA
