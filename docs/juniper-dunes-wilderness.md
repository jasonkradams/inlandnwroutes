## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
​Juniper Dunes is a peaceful 7140 acre paradise in the sand. Although there are no trails in the Wilderness, wandering the dunes and admiring the environment will astound you. Use extra caution when setting out. Look for features and triangulate far off mountains, etc.
Contact the BLM office in Spokane for details on road access, as they change as often as the sands, it seems. BLM will be able to tell you when the wildflowers are in bloom.
If you are able to wander, wander the area with care. There are few landmarks to go by, so you may want to take your compass and map to be sure.
There is a dense stand of Juniper along the north boundary near a 100+ foot dune.

## Directions

Take I-90 west to Ritzville and turn left (SW) onto US 395. past Connell to Mesa. Turn left (east) and work your way to Juniper Dunes.
Because access changes, please contact the Spokane office of the BLM for details and any current restrictions.

## Hazards

As in any wilderness, you must be careful to keep track of where you are at all times. Carry your 13 essentials for safety.
On the NW boarder of the wilderness, is an ORV park, so if you are looking for solitude, avoid the NW section.
