# Hiking Boise

## About Me

### Greg Hart

I’m a 2x Boise State alumni and financial analyst living in Idaho with a passion for exploring and protecting the wilderness around me. I’ve hiked nearly 1,000 miles in the last two years, mostly within a few hours of my home in southeast Boise, and I’m enthusiastic about sharing my experiences so that others may go out and enjoy nature in the same way. I started this blog so I could share insider knowledge on some of the less-traveled places around Boise for beginners and well-seasoned hikers alike.

I actively volunteer for [Idaho Trails Association](https://idahotrailsassociation.org/), [Oregon Natural Desert Association](https://onda.org/), and the [Idaho Foundation for Parks and Lands](https://idaholands.org/), and I’d encourage other trail users to offer some of their time to great organizations like these.
