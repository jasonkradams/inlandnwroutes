## Description

The Dorr Skeel Camp Ground is located at the north end of Bull Lake. The Dorr Skeel C. G. is large, with many camp site, some on the shore line.
​The C. G. launch is located on the south end of the C.G.

## Attractions

This C. G. is located on the more quiet end of Bull Lake.

## Directions

From the Hwy 200 and Hwy 56 junction, drive about 21.4 miles to the Dorr Skeel C. G. turn off.

## Cool things close by

The Halfway House Bar, the Cabinet Mountain Wilderness, Kootenai Falls, and Libby.
