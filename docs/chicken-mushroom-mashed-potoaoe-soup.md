## Chicken mushroom mashed potato soup

---

This is a quick and easy dish to make.

Ingredients: Organic potatoes 2 sprigs of salad onions, chopped 4 Regular button mushrooms, sliced. Onions
optional Baked chicken thighs and breasts and cook your own, or buy chicken from a deli

Debone the chicken, and boil the bones.

Boil the potatoes while frying the mushrooms and salad onion in butter. I always make much more mashed
potatoes than I need. In the morning I fry them and top them with an egg.

Remove all chicken solids and place the broth in a frying pan. Cook at a medium heat.

Take a 6-8 ounce jar with a lid. Put an inch or two niches of regular flour in the jar. My grandmother
taught me how to make gravy way back in the 60’s. She insisted that she only adds ice cold water to the
flour in the jar. Cap it and shake thoroughly. Using a fork, slowly pour in the flour-water mixture, always
mixing vigorously with a fork.

When the gravy sets up, add salt, pepper, garlic powder and the fried mushrooms, onions and chicken.

Mash the potatoes with about an ounce of butter or oleo. Do not add milk. And, do not over boil the potatoes
to mush. If they get too mushy, they aren’t the best.

To serve, divide up the mashed potatoes into medium size glass bowls. Then add the gravy mix over the
potatoes.

Hence Chicken, Mushroom, Mashed Potatoes, Soup.

Enjoy. If you don’t take anything else out of this recipe, ALWAYS follow the gravy instructions to the T.
The recipe is under RECIPE....EASY QUALITY GRAVY..... Only add other ingredients after the gravy has
thickened. It will never fail you. c.
