## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
The Anderson Lake boat launch is located on Hwy 97 as it crosses the CDA River NE of Harrison, Idaho. Across the CDA River NE of the Hwy 97 bridge, is an inlet to Anderson Lake. As you paddle the lake, Bell Canyon sup a short cove.
​From this launch you can paddle out into the main body of Lake CDA via there CDA River.

## Attractions

Easy launch site to visit several options on the river, and a paddle to CDA Lake.

## Directions

From Spokane, drive east on I-90 over 4th of July Pass, and turn right (S), onto Hwy 3, the Rose Lake exit. Drive SW  on Hwy 3 to the junction with Hwy 97. Turn right (NW) on Hwy 97 thru Harrison, to the Hwy 97 bridge and launch on the north side of the river.
