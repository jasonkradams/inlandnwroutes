## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
​The trailhead starts about .5 miles up the Cedar Creek Trail #141.

Turn right (NE) for a short distance until you start the seven switchbacks. At the top switchback, the trail climbs steadily to the ridge line that leads to the summit. Most all of this trail skirts a burn area from 1994. Once on the summit, visit the fire lookout area for views that give this mountain its name.

---

## Directions

About 4 miles west of Libby, look for the Cedar Creek Road #402 sign on the SW side of Hwy 2. The trailhead is 2.5 mikes up #402.

## Hazards

4200’ of elevation gain. There is no water past the Cedar Creek Trail turnoff.

## Cool things close by

Cedar Lakes, William Grambauer Mt., Taylor Peak, and Dome Mt.
