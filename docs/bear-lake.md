# Bear Lake

## Description

In a little less than 3 miles, the trail enters the wilderness. Near this boundary, look for an obscure trail heading north to Bear Lake. There are 3 lakes in the Bear Lakes group. A small lake is located NE of Bear Lake, and NNW is a faint trail to Upper Bear Lake.

## Route Options

### Option #1

Bear Lakes can be accessed via the Geiger Lakes trail and the Cabinet Divide Trail #360.

## Directions

About 20 miles south of Libby on Hwy 2 is Sedlak Park. Turn south onto Silver Butte Fisher River Road #148 for 10 miles to the trailhead.

## Hazards

Trail #531 actually goes to the Cabinet Divide Trail #360. At the CMW boundary, look for a faint path north to Bear Lake. There is no water along the trail until you get to the lake.

## Cool Things Close By

Cabinet Divide Trail, Geiger Lakes, Baree Mountain, Noxon Mt., Clark Fork River, Bull Lake & River, Ross Creek Cedars.

## Rest & Provisions

Clark Fork Pantry & Squeeze Inn in Clark Fork; Henry’s & Pizza Hut in Libby; Eichardt's, Mr. Sub & Jalapeños in Sandpoint.
