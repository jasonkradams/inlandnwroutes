# Albeni Cove Launch

## Description

Albeni Cove is located next to the Albeni Dam just east of Newport, on the Pend Orielle River.
The campgrounds offer 14 camp sites, flush toilets, picnic tables, showers, a boat ramp and dock.
The Pend Orielle River stretches 65 miles downstream and 27 miles upstream to the Long Bridge at Sandpoint.

## Attractions

The Pend Orielle River meanders through a wide valley as it stretches from Sandpoint to the Boundary Dam
north of Metaline Falls, in N. Washington. Views of the American Selkirks, Sullivan Lake, Boundary Dam,
and Pewee Falls.

## Directions

From Spokane, drive north to Newport. At Newport, head east on Hwy 2 for less than 2 miles to the
Albeni Dam site.
