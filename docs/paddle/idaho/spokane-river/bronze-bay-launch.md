# Bronze Bay Launch

## Description

Bronze Bay Launch is a public boat ramp located on the western shore of Hayden Lake in Kootenai County,
Idaho. The launch provides direct paddlecraft access to Hayden Lake's 1,445 surface acres, scenic forested
bays, and quiet residential coves.

## Access & Directions

From Coeur d'Alene, drive north on US-95 to Hayden, ID. Turn right (east) onto W. Hayden Avenue, then left
(north) onto N. Government Way. Turn right (east) onto E. Lancaster Road, follow it to N. Strahorn Road, and
continue north to E. Bronze Bay Road and the boat launch site.
