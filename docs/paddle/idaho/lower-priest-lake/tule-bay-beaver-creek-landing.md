# Tule Bay / Beaver Creek Landing

## Directions

From Priest River, Idaho, take Hwy 57 north to Nordman, then turn right on Reeder Bay Road and continue for 12 miles to the campgrounds.

## Nearby Attractions

- Indian Creek Campgrounds
- Upper Priest Lake
- Thorough Fare to Upper Priest Lake
- Priest Lake State Park (Lionshead Unit)
- Granite Falls
- Blacktail Mountain
