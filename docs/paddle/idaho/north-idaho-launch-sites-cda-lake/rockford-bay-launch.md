# Rockford Bay Launch

![Rockford Bay Launch](../../../assets/images/2021520504_orig.jpeg)
_Rockford Bay Launch_

## Description

The cool thing about this launch, is that it access the southern section of Lake CDA. After paddling out of
the bay, Rockford Point sticks out into the lake. Further south is Sun Up Bay, Windy Bay

## Attractions

The bay is large and will take you out into the body of Lake CDA

## Directions

From CDA, drive south on Hwy 95 to the Bellgrove turn off, and continue east on W. Rockford Bay Road, to the
Shooters Bar & Grill. The launch is just past the marina.

## Cool Things Close By

Sun Up Bay, Windy Bay, and the Harrison Slough

## Restaurants & Pubs

Trails End Brewery, Franklins, Moon Time, Mexican Food Factory
