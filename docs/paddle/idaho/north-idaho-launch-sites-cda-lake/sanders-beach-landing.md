# Sanders Beach Landing

![Sanders Beach Landing](../../../assets/images/2020916647_orig.png)
_Sanders Beach Landing_

## Description

Sanders Beach is a private beach with two public access points at 11th Street and 15th Street. If the west
put in is crowded, drive a few blocks east to the 15th Street access. From Sanders Beach, you can paddle east
towards the CDA Resort's floating green and on east end of the north shore. Or you can paddle west along
Tubbs Hill and around to the south shore and CDA.

## Attractions

Wide open water, Tubbs Hill, the CDA Golf Course, Wolf Lodge Bay, Cougar Bay, and the Spokane River.

## Directions

Drive east on I-90 to the 15th Street exit, and head south to Sanders Beach.

See Attractions above.
