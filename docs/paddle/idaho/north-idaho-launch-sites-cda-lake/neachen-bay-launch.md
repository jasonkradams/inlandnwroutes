# Neachen Bay Launch

![Neachen Bay Launch](../../../assets/images/20200917063953_orig.jpg)
_Neachen Bay Launch_

## Description

Neachen Bay used to be called Squaw Bay if you are looking at older maps. The launch is located up near Wolf
Lodge Bay. You will feel like you are driving into private residences, but eventually the launch appears.

## Attractions

This launch gets you out onto the main body of the lake via Arrow Point, and has access to Wolf Lodge Bay's
west end.

## Directions

From CDA drive east on I-90 to the Harrison, Idaho exit, onto Hwy 97A. Drive west on Hwy 97A above Beauty
Bay. There is a rest area just east of the road to Eddyville. Turn right (North) onto the Eddyville Road, and
stay on this road all the way to the launch.

## Cool Things Close By

The main body of Lake CDA, Wolf Lodge Bay, Moscow Bay, Arrow Point Resort.

## R & P

Trails End Brewery, Franklins, Mexican Food Factory, and Moon Time.
