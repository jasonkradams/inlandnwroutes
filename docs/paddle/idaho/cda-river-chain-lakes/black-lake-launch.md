# Black Lake Launch

## Description

We have added the area's sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district
info. If an emergency occurs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.

**East Access:** Rainy Hill Launch and Campgrounds are east of Black Lake on E. Rainy Hill Road.

**West Access:** The launch is located on E. Blue Lake Road and services Thompson Lake also.

!!! warning "River Current Notice"
    Always take into account the flow of the river. From either launch, you will have to paddle upstream
    coming or going.

## Attractions

CdA River, Swan Lake, Blue Lake, Cave Lake, Medicine Lake, and Harrison, Idaho.

## Directions

### East Launch (Rainy Hill)

Drive east on I-90 over 4th of July Pass and turn right (south) onto Hwy 3 towards Harrison. Just past the
Killarney Lake turnoff, and before Medicine Lake, turn right onto E. Rainy Hill Road for about 0.7 miles to
the launch.

### West Launch (Thompson Lake)

After turning south onto Hwy 3 at Rose Lake, continue to Harrison, then over the CdA River. Turn right (NE)
onto E. Blue Lake Road to the Thompson Lake launch.

## Cool Things Close By

Harrison Lake, Anderson Lake, CdA River, and Lake CdA.
