# Old Mission Launch

## Description

The Old Mission Launch is located adjacent to Interstate 90 at Old Mission State Park along the Coeur
d'Alene River near Cataldo, Idaho. Paddling upstream from the boat ramp leads toward shallow riffles just
below the I-90 bridge, above which paddlers enjoy smooth, quiet water depending on river levels.

Paddling downstream offers scenic access through the lower Coeur d'Alene River valley toward the Chain
Lakes. Due to steady river currents, downstream paddlers should arrange a shuttle vehicle at a lower
pullout. The launch facility features a concrete ramp and ample vehicle parking.

## Key Attractions

- Quiet river paddling with minimal shoreline development
- Access to the historic Old Mission State Park and Cataldo Mission
- Gateway to the Coeur d'Alene River Chain Lakes system (Rose, Killarney, Swan, Cave, Black, Blue, Thompson,
  and Anderson Lakes)

## Access & Directions

From Coeur d'Alene, drive east on I-90 to the Old Mission State Park exit (Exit 40). Turn right (south) into
the park, then turn right onto Dredge Road and follow it to the end of the road at the river launch.
