## Description

The Q’emlin Park Boat Launch is located south of the Post Falls Dam on the south side of the Spokane Bridge over the Spokane River. The launch has 2 lanes, plenty of parking, picnic area with tables and a swimming beach.
You can only paddle up stream because of the Post Falls Dam. The launch is located 9 miles from Lake CDA, and offers great paddling all the way to the lake.

## Attractions

Besides the launch and the water park amenities, Q’emlin has over 500 acres of hiking, and dozens of rock climbing routes.

## Directions

Drive east from Spokane to the Spokane Street exit. Turn right (south) and continue over the bridge and river, to the first right (west) turn. The park is on your right.
