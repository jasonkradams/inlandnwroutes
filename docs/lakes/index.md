# Alpine & Regional Lakes of the Inland Northwest

!!! info "Quick Facts: Lakes & Waterways"

    - **Major Ranges:** Selkirk Crest, Cabinet Mountains Wilderness, Bitterroot Range, Coeur d'Alene
      Mountains
    - **Lake Types:** High-alpine glacial tarns, alpine cirque basins, lowland lakes, river reservoirs
    - **Activities:** Day hiking, alpine scrambling, backcountry camping, fishing, kayaking & paddling
    - **Featured Guides:** Beehive Lake, Harrison Lake, Fault Lake, Wanless Lake, Upper & Lower Stevens
      Lakes

## Description

The Inland Northwest is home to an extraordinary variety of freshwater lakes. Tucked deep within granite
amphitheaters high on the Selkirk Crest, carved into the dramatic vertical walls of the Cabinet Mountains
Wilderness, or nestling among the lush timber of the Bitterroot Range, these lakes range from remote alpine
tarns accessible only via strenuous off-trail scrambles to peaceful lowland waters ideal for kayaking and
family outings.

Whether you are seeking a multi-day backcountry loop to crystal-clear alpine waters or a day trip to explore
coastal paddle launches, the guides below detail trail lengths, elevation gains, trailhead directions, and
local safety considerations.
