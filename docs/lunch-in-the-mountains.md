# Lunch In The Mountains

## Lunch in the mountains (2)

---

Ingredients:

Each of the vegetable ingredients should be washed, and cut appropriately, and stored in individual leak
proof containers. Hoagie Rolls. Pick the softest best bread you can find Lunch meat. Prima Della is best
Organic Romaine lettuce Organic baby spinach Cherry tomatoes cut into small pieces at home. Avocado Mayo
made with olive oil Salt & pepper Chips of your choice Cookies for dessert Libations as desired A red and
white checkered table cloth. And two knives.

As stated above, put all ingredients in separate leak proof containers. Each recipient of lunch can carry a
portion, so no one has to carry it all. And besides, each person should have their own food, water, and
trail snacks.

Lunch in the mountains: Spread the table cloth out on a flat surface, and put a rock on each corner. Lay out
the ingredients, and have knives and firm paper plates available.

Have each person build their sandwich as they desire. I always carry enough ingredients, for emergency
needs.

Enjoy
