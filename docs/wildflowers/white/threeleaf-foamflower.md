# Threeleaf Foamflower

![Threeleaf Foamflower](../../assets/images/11142021533_73.jpg)
_Threeleaf Foamflower_

## Description

*Tiarella trifoliata* (Threeleaf foamflower) is a species of flowering plant in the saxifrage family. It
produces tiny white flowers hanging in loose clusters in narrow branched racemes atop leafy stems.
