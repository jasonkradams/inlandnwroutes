# Fern & Shadow Falls and Centennial Falls

## Description

Fern & Shadow Falls Trail #513 leads to two falls close together in the same drainage. The road to
these falls is in poor condition and requires a high-clearance vehicle. As you approach, Fern Falls is
the first falls you see. Stop here and photograph the falls from all around; the creek below with the
falls in the background offers some great shots. To reach Shadow Falls, follow the path on the left up
to Shadow. This falls is taller, and you can walk behind it. On a hot day, it's a nice shower to cool
off.

### Centennial Falls

Centennial Falls is really only impressive during heavy runoff, but as long as you are at Fern & Shadow
Falls, Centennial Falls is in the vicinity and worth the detour.

## Directions

### Fern & Shadow Falls (Trail #513)

The road to these falls is in poor condition and requires a high-clearance vehicle. From Coeur d'Alene,
Idaho, drive east on I-90, taking the Kingston Exit #43. After exiting, travel north on Forest Highway 9
(FH9) for 23.1 miles to Forest Road #208 (FR208). Continue on FR208 for 14.4 miles to FR2367, Beetle
Creek Road. From the turn-off on FR2367, follow the signs to Shadow Falls. It is approximately 4.5 miles
to the 7-car parking lot located at the trailhead.

### Centennial Falls (Trail #400)

Heading east on I-90, take the Kingston Exit and drive 39 miles on the CDA River Road. Immediately after
the 39-mile marker, take a left onto Flat Creek Road #400. Stay on this bumpy road and follow it over two
bridges. You will find the trailhead on a corner with a sign posted.

## Cool Things Close By

Little Guard Lookout, Spion Top Rocks, CDA River N.R.T., Bloom Peak, and Settlers Grove of Ancient
Cedars.

## Hazards

All waterfalls are a hazard due to their slippery nature. Always be extra careful near any waterfall.
