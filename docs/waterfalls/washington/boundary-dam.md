# Boundary Dam

![Relaxing beside a forested lake near Boundary Dam](../../assets/images/img-1826-97.jpg)
_Relaxing beside a forested lake near Boundary Dam._

## Description

Boundary Dam is a Seattle City Light hydroelectric dam on the Pend Oreille River, about a mile south of the
Canadian border near Metaline Falls, WA. It's a concrete arch-gravity dam, 338' tall and 740' long, completed
in 1967, with its powerhouse built into a limestone cavern below the canyon rim. The dam and its reservoir
sit right in the middle of the same Pend Oreille River trip that leads to Pewee Falls, so it's an easy add-on
whether you're paddling, camping, or just passing through on the drive up Hwy 31.

### Guided Tours

Seattle City Light runs free, one-hour guided tours of the Machine Hall Visitor Gallery deep inside the
dam, July 3 through August, Friday through Monday at 8 AM, 10 AM, 12:30 PM, and 2:30 PM. Tours are
first-come, first-served for up to 25 people, meet at the Boundary Dam guard station, and require photo ID
for adults and closed-toe shoes. Groups of 10 or more should call or email ahead.

## Directions

From Newport, WA, head north on Hwy 20 to Tiger, WA. At Tiger, turn right (N) onto Hwy 31 and continue north
past Metaline Falls. The Boundary Dam guard station and access road are about 12 miles up from Hwy 31 near
the town of Metaline.

## Cool Things Close By

The Forebay Recreation Area below the dam has a free 22-site campground (6-night limit), day-use picnic
areas, and a concrete boat launch with an accessible dock -- the same launch used to paddle up to Pewee
Falls. Metaline Falls Portage Park adds a paved 1,600-foot overlook trail with restrooms, open year-round
from sunrise to sunset. Upstream on the reservoir, the Pewee Falls and Riverside Canyon overlooks (opened
2023) have their own parking, restrooms, and an emerging 9-mile trail. Gardner Cave at Crawford State Park
and Sullivan Lake are both a short drive north and west of the dam.

## Hazards

Swift, cold water below the dam and steep drop-offs along the canyon rim are the main hazards. Stay behind
railings at the overlooks, and follow posted rules on tour days -- photo ID and closed-toe shoes are
required to enter the Machine Hall Visitor Gallery.

## Restaurants & Pubs

N/A -- the dam sits in a remote stretch of the Pend Oreille valley. The nearest food and fuel are in
Metaline Falls and Ione.
