# Finch Arboretum

![Garden Springs Creek runs through the arboretum](../../assets/images/11052021442a.jpg)
_Garden Springs Creek runs through the arboretum._

## Description

The John A. Finch Arboretum is one of the most beautiful parks in the Spokane area, and should not be
missed. All seasons are incredible, but early Rocktober, before the city rakes up the fallen leaves, is my
favorite: spring at the Arboretum is well worth a visit, and as the colors start to change in fall, the
trees turn vibrant. Garden Springs Creek cascades often through the grounds, and by late fall the grass is
carpeted in yellow leaves, with several places to sit and watch the day go by; as winter approaches, the
creek freezes around its many cascades.

Planting began in 1949 with 49 specimens comprising 10 genera and 23 species. Today, the arboretum houses
over 2,000 labeled trees and shrubs representing over 600 species and varieties spanning 120 genera. The
Arboretum occupies nearly 65 acres of tree-covered land along Garden Springs Creek in the southwest part of
Spokane. Its collections include natives of the Inland Northwest and plants from many parts of the world,
selected for their educational, scientific, and aesthetic value. The Arboretum is used as an outdoor
classroom by naturalists, horticulturists, students, gardeners, and photographers, and those interested in
home landscaping will find it a valuable source of information on plants hardy enough to grow in the
Spokane area. Seasonal programs and public events throughout the year enhance its educational and
scientific value, and its park-like character and natural charm are appreciated by all who enjoy nature's
beauty.

Hungry for more history? Check out John A. Finch Arboretum by Tracy Rebstock on SpokaneHistorical.org.

## Directions

From I-90, take the Garden Springs Road Exit 277, and turn north off the Interstate. Turn right at the stop
sign onto W. Sunset Blvd, head down hill towards Spokane, and be in the right hand lane. Turn right onto
"F" Street to the Woodland Center and parking (509.363.5466).

## Cool Things Close By

Palisades Falls, the Spokane River, and Riverside State Park.

## Hazards

Pets are not allowed in the Arboretum.
