# Palisades Creek Falls

## Description

The Palisades Falls are located in Chief Garry Park. The falls are tucked back into an area that may make
it hard to find.

## Directions

From the intersection of Government Way and Sunset Blvd, drive north on Government Way to Greenwood Road,
and turn left (W). In a mile bear right at a fork with Indian Canyon Road. Then turn left on a gravel road
to parking spots. While at the parking area, walk north for a great view of downtown Spokane. Hike Trail
101 to a junction with Trail 122 and go left.

## Cool Things Close By

Indian Canyon Golf Course, John A. Finch Arboretum, Riverside State Park, and the Spokane River.

## Hazards

All waterfalls are a hazard, due to their slippery nature. Always be extra careful near any waterfall.
