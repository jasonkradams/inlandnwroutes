# Canada Outdoor Routes & Regional Guide

## Overview & Regional Topography

Just north of the U.S. border in British Columbia and Alberta lie some of North America's most spectacular
mountain ranges. The Purcell Mountains, the Canadian Rockies, and the Kootenay region offer world-renowned
granite spires, glaciers, alpine lakes, and premier ski resorts.

!!! warning "Glacier & Backcountry Safety"

    Mountaineering and backcountry travel in the Purcells and Canadian Rockies require experienced
    navigation, ice axes, crampons, and crevasse rescue training. Summer weather can change rapidly with
    sudden lightning, hail, and snowstorms at high elevations.

---

## Bugaboo Provincial Park & Mountaineering

Situated in the rugged Purcell Mountains west of Highway 95 between Golden and Radium Hot Springs, **Bugaboo
Provincial Park** encompasses over 13,000 hectares of ice fields, ancient glaciers, and sheer granite spires
exceeding 3,000 meters in elevation.

### Climbing & Alpine Highlights

- **Famous Spires:** Bugaboo Spire, Howser Towers (North Howser), Snowpatch Spire, Crescent Spire, and
  Pigeon Spire.
- **Historic Ascent:** Pioneer guide Conrad Kain achieved the first ascent of Bugaboo Spire's South Ridge
in 1916.
- **Conrad Kain Hut:** Maintained by the Alpine Club of Canada (ACC), accommodating up to 35 climbers below
  Bugaboo Glacier (reservations required June 1 to September 30).

### Key Hiking & Approach Trails

- **Conrad Kain Hut Trail:** Strenuous 4.5 km trail ascending northern lateral moraines of Bugaboo Glacier.
- **Cobalt Lake Trail:** Steep trail to an open alpine ridge with panoramic spire views, descending to
  Cobalt Lake.
- **Malloy Igloo Trail:** Backcountry route from Kain Hut across glaciers to Malloy Igloo (roped parties
  required).
