# Blossom Lake

## Lower & Upper Blossom Lakes (ID/MT Border)

## Description

We have added the area's sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district
info. If an emergency occurs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.

For some reason the Lolo N.F. and the I.P.N.F. do not list these and other trails on either website along
the Idaho/Montana border. So there is not an official distance or elevation gain/loss available. All other
numbers are strictly USFS unless noted.

The trail starts at Thompson Pass (4840') parking area on a fairly level trail for about 0.5 miles. From
here the trail starts a gradual climb to the high point at 5775'. The rest of the trail descends slowly to
the lake. The trail goes through a rocky area just before it reaches Lower Blossom Lake. Along the way, look
for an old diversion trench dug by Chinese laborers to supply water to mining operations (which was never
used).

## Route Options

### Option #1: Upper Blossom Lake

From the lower lake, you can walk back to the trail and continue about a mile to a junction. Bear right over
to Upper Blossom Lake. The walk over to U.B.L. is through a nice easy forest. There is a small campsite next
to the exit creek. Retrace your steps back to the junction.

### Option #2: Pear Lake

From U.B.L., walk back to the main trail and turn right at the junction up to Pear Lake. Look for a wooden
sign. It’s about a mile to Pear Lake, but the trail undulates on its ascent. Pear Lake sits below a 500'
cliff that is very picturesque, and the whole lake area is very sensitive. Please use clean camping
techniques, and dispose of human and dog waste AT LEAST 200 FEET FROM ANY WATER SOURCE, TRAIL, OR CAMPSITE.

### Option #3: Ridge Scramble to Revett Lake

From Lower Blossom Lake, go to the back right (west) end of the lake and scramble the scree slope up to the
low notch on the ridge. In front of you will be Revett Lake and the trail back to the cars. Follow the scree
slope to the NE to the lake. This extension is altogether about a 9-mile loop. There is no trail between the
two lakes, so good route-finding skills are a must.

## Directions

Drive I-90 east to the Kingston exit #43, and turn left (north) over the freeway onto FR #9 (also known as
the Coeur d'Alene River Road). Drive north for about 21 miles and bear right (east) at Prichard, then
continue for about 16 miles to Thompson Pass at the Idaho/Montana border. Off to the right at the summit of
the road, you will see a parking area. The trailhead is near the south corner of the parking area.

## Hazards

All the trails from the trailhead to Pear Lake are in great shape, but always use caution.

## Cool Things Close By

Revett Lake, Coeur d'Alene River Trail #20, Granite Peak, The Idaho Centennial Trail.

## Rest & Provisions

The Snake Pit, The Snag Pole Bar & Museum.
