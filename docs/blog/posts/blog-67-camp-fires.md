Please everyone having campfires is a huge responsibility.

Since 6.14.2024 i have put out 3 smoldering campfires left by hikers/backpackers.
Please do two things for each hike, camptrip
If you must have a campfire, carry 5-8 plastic grocery bags.
They only weigh 6 grams per bag, and could save our forests.
Use them for carrying water to extinguish your fire.And, if you come upon a hot campfire, please use the bags
to carry
water to extinguish any hot ashes.
They tend to rip after many loads, hence 5-8 bags.
It would be ashame to see stevens lake  look like, after the big burn.
