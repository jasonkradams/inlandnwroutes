## Othello Sandhill Crane Festival

The Othello Sandhill Crane Festival is about a lot more than just cranes. If you love nature and are
interested in birds, geology, plants, and wildlife, this is a great way to meet like-minded people while
attending seminars and tours led by experts who have spent their lives studying these topics. The daily schedule
is packed with parallel tours and talks over several days.

Othello is a unique location surrounded by farm lands for grains, fruit, and onions. It sits in the heart of
fascinating Eastern Washington geography: Potholes State Park, Vantage, Hanford Reach, Saddle Mountains, Columbia
National Wildlife Refuge, Ginkgo Petrified Forest State Park, and West Crab Creek are all less than an hour away.

## The Cranes

The first activity was a guided tour to view the cranes. We boarded buses with a wildlife biologist who explained
how local farmers leave corn stalks in their fields for migrating birds across 800+ acres. The guides knew the exact
roosting sites, giving us front-row views of small flocks landing at sunset against the evening sky.

## Birding & Local Parks

Guided birding tours took us through local parks densely populated with mature evergreen trees—a stark contrast
to the surrounding desert and irrigated farmland. Owls roost in the evergreens and hunt in the adjacent fields.
The tour leader, Michael, shared incredible insights into species behavior and habitat dynamics.
