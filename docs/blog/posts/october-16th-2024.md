Technical clothing and water proofing

At one time or another, we all get wet in the mountains.
Whether you are hiking, climbing, paddling, or enjoying winter sports, we all need to waterproof our outer layers.

But there are hazards to using some products.
Some waterproofing sprays contains PFAS, also known as Forever Chemicals.
Please do your research. I can’t name them, but you can Google products that are not safe to use.

How to Water Proof Your Clothes and other gear.

The way I have been treating my technical gear for decades is below.
You may have other methods, and that okay.
What ever works for you is okay, as long as you know some important facts.

First I wash my gear one by one.
I use powdered Arm & Hammer laundry soap.
Detergents that are not powdered, may contain additives that may affect your garments water proofing  abilities.

I wash my garment, then rewash the garment WITHOUT any laundry soap.

While the garment is still wet, I spray it with a PFAS FREE Durable Water Resistant spray.
Make sure you cover your garment thoroughly. And then spray areas again like shoulders, back, seat, and legs or arms.

Then the garment goes in the dryer for at least 30 minute.
If it’s not thoroughly dry, hang and let dry.
Just don’t spray and go.
The DWR’s need to blend with the material.

As the ski or hiking season progresses, you may have to retreat your garments.
Respray it wet or dry, and use your dryer to "cure" the garment.

Products that do not contain PFAS are Durable Water Resistant sprays are listed below:
Nikwax DWR
Gearaid dwr
Granger dwr

All these manufacturers make a special Wash.
If you don’t have the Arm & Hammer laundry soap, consider one of the above brands.
However, Arm & Hammer cost a fraction of the above brands.

A word about these pump spray applicators…
They are difficult to spray on evenly.
What I do, is spray my garments lightly twice.
Then while wearing a plastic glove, I rub the DWR into the cloth, before drying them. That way, I know it’s spread evenly.

If you buy all the Arm & Hammer and the DWR sprays  above now, you can get a jump on late Fall early Winter physical training, and ski season.
