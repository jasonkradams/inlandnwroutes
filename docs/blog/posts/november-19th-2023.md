## Why Everyone Should Carry a Map & Compass

The biggest reason to always have a map & compass and the knowledge to use them is... batteries die. If you
rely only on your phone or GPS unit to get you around in the forest, you may be putting yourself and others
at risk. Besides, a map & compass weighs a fraction of even a GPS unit, and they do not require batteries.

When safety is paramount, you will learn that having the knowledge and equipment to get you through the night
is your best and only option. When I lead hikes for the Spokane Mountaineers, I make copies of the USFS Forest
Map and Topo map showing details of our driving and hiking routes. Each person gets a copy.

And to further this suggestion, **each person should carry the 13 Essentials in their own pack**. Your pack
should also include food, first aid kit, water, rain gear, and any other items you feel are necessities.

Keep your 13 Essentials light and restock items as you use them. As fall temperatures drop, we all must carry
clothing for all weather conditions. Don't forget stocking caps and warm gloves. To check if you are prepared,
put on what you normally wear on a hike and stand outside an hour before bed. Stay still and see how you feel
after an hour, then realize you may have 6+ hours until sunrise if stranded.

Please plan ahead, and have too much fun.
