# Tcda Curried Rice

## Tcda curried rice (2)

---

Ingredients:

1 Large onion 2 stocks of celery with leaves 1.5 zucchini sliced 1/2 C chopped red peppers 2 C broth 1.5 C
frozen corn 2 C water 1 C spaghetti sauce 1 T of your favorite curry 1 T need curry paste

Cook all the above ingredients until veggies are done. Cover a plate with Spinach, and pour above over the
spinach. Then add spinach to the top. Enjoy
