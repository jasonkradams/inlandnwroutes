# Garfield Bay Launch

## Description

The Garfield Bay Launch is located near the Garfield Bay Camp Ground.
Once out of Garfield Bay, to the left is Mineral Point (Trail).
To the right is Talache Landing.

## Attractions

BIG water paddling, Mineral Point Hiking Trail, and Green Bay Boat Camp.

## Directions

Drive north on 95 to Sagle and turn right (East) onto the Sagle Road, and drive about 7.5 miles, staying on Garfield Bay Road, to the launch

## Cool things close by

Talache Landing, Shepherd Lake, and Mirror Lake. If the winds are up on P.O.Lake, these two small lakes are an option

## R & p

MR. Sub, Eichardt’s, jalapeños, and Burger Express in Sandpoint
