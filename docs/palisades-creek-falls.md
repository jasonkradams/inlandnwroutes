# Palisades Creek Falls

## Palisades creek falls

---

WATERFALL : Palisades Creek Falls
DROP: About 30'
WATERFALL TYPE: Tiered
​DISTANCE CAR TO FALLS: 4.2 mile loop
MAPS: Spokane County, Spokane SW
Gps:  47°39’28” n 117°29’?17” w

---

## Description

The Palisades Falls are located in the Chief Garry Park. 
The falls are tucked back into an area that may make it hard to find.

---

## Directions

From the intersection of Government Way and Sunset Blvd, drive north on Government Way to Greenwood Roa, and turn left (W). In a mile bear right at a fork with Indian Canyon Road. Then turn left on a gravel road to parking spots.
While at the parking area, walk north for a great view of downtown Spokane.
Hike Trail 101 to a junction with Trail 122 and go left. Trail 122.

---

## Cool things close by

Indian Canyon Golf Course, John A. Finch Arboretum, Riverside State Park, and the Spokane River.

## Hazards

All waterfalls are a hazard, due to their slippery nature. always be extra careful near any waterfall.
​ From Priest Lake Ranger District travel 4 miles north to Nordman, then 14 miles north of Nordman, Idaho
