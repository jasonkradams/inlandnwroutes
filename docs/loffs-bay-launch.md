# Loffs Bay Launch

## Description

We have added the areas sheriff’s emergency phone numbers for each trip write up under the ranger district info. if an emergency ocurrs, evaluate your circumstances and call only if needed.
Loft’s Bay is along the west side of Lake CDA. It is an easy way to get out to the misnaming body of Lake CDA.
On the right shore line as the bay enters the main body, is Camp Cross.

## Attractions

An easy way to get onto the lake. Way less crowded and very scenic

## Directions

From CDA, turn right onto Hwy 95, and drive south from the Spokane River Bridge for 13 miles to the S. Rockford Bay Road. Drive eastish for 1.24 miles and bear left onto S. Loft’s Bay Road. Stay on S. Loft’s Bay Road all the way to Loft’s Bay.
Its a long and windy road, but just stay on S. Loft’s Bay Road to the launch.

## Cool things close by

Rockford Bay, Mica Bay, and the main body of Lake CDA

## R & P

Trails End Brewery, Mexican Food Factory, Franklins Hoagies, and Moon Time
