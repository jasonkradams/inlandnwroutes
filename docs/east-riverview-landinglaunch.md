# East Riverview Landinglaunch

*West riverview launch*

## Description

This Landing is very small and does not have adequate area to turn a boat trailer around, and no concrete ramp. Look for a small brown sign pointing to the landing driveway. The water from the landing is very shallow and will not take medium to large boats. There is inadequate parking, and if you park on W. Riverview, DO NOT ALLOW YOUR WHEELS TO BE ON THE ROADWAY. There is a port a potty on site.
please respect the neighbors privacy, and property.

## Attractions

Good landing for canoes and kayaks, and flat water river paddling

## Directions

From I-90 and the Post Falls, take the Spokane Street exit and drive over the Spokane River. Continue past Q’Emlin Park to the first left (East) turn onto W. Riverview Dr. Continue East on W. Riverview Dr for 1.7 miles, and watch for the small launch sign.

## Cool things close by

Q’Emlin Park, Post Falls Dam and Park and climbing rocks

## R & p

Franklins Hoagies, the Moon Time, the Mexican Food Factory, and the Trails End Brewery.
