# [Hiking from here](https://www.hikingfromhere.com/index.html)

Welcome to my website dedicated to hiking for those who live in or near Lewiston and Moscow in Idaho, or Clarkston and Pullman in Washington – 100 miles near, as the crow flies, most of the time.

**Hiking From Here** contains descriptions of all the hikes I have taken in Washington, Oregon and Idaho. Links to the descriptions can be found on the [Hikes](https://www.hikingfromhere.com/hikes.html) page. You also can view some beautiful photos taken on the hikes by visiting my [Photos](https://www.hikingfromhere.com/photos.html) page.

Please try out some of the hikes you find here! And [Contact](https://www.hikingfromhere.com/contact.html) me if you'd like more information.
