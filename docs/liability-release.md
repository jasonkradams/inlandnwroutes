# Liability Release

## Liability and user release

We use the honor system

It is our goal to supply the most correct and up to date information on trails, skis, paddles, etc., and alternative routes we can, in our region.

We will correct or add as it comes in from you, the users, after we research
your submission

When you read the hike distance and elevation gain and loss,
these numbers are from the U.S.F.S., B.L.M.
or the areas MANAGING AGENCY
They are accurate. Rely on them.
However, if you see the word "about",
the numbers are from personal GPS readings.

Please understand that the directions to the event and related data are as correct as we see it in the field. I use GOOGLE EARTH for my measurement estimations, because when you use G.E., it’s the same for you as it is for me. But be aware, no GPS unit will match another on the same trip. But I use the USFS data in the Elevation and Distance for the hike.

However, changes are made to the driving routes as well as outings locations and routes, without our knowledge. Therefore, we can not be held responsible for changes made without our knowledge.

We also state that none of the enclosed information is gospel.

But we will do our best to supply you with the most current information,
maps, and activity data we can.

Which brings you into the picture. We ask you to contact us if you find any information that is outdated, incorrect, changed, or altered in any way.

We will in turn, research your comments, additions and criticisms, and edit our data to match those ever changing conditions in the field.
