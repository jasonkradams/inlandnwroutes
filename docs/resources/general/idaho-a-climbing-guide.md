# Idaho A Climbing Guide

## IDAHO: A Climbing Guide | Mountaineering in Idaho

Welcome to Idaho: A Climbing Guide–The Website This website supplements Idaho: A Climbing Guide. The site
includes updated access and route information, new peaks, new routes, photos, maps and information provided
by myself and many [Contributors](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/book-update-contributors/).

Please read the [Using the Website](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/using-the-website/) page for tips on
getting the most out of the Peak pages. Please read the
[Route Ratings](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/ratings-game/) page to understand how routes are rated.
Please read
[Trespassing and the Idaho Code, A Primer](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/private-property-idaho-code-trespassing-%e2%80%a8/),
to help keep your climbs legal. Please read our
[Be Careful](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/climbing-safety/) and
[Disclaimer](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/disclaimer/) pages to help you stay mindful that climbing
is a dangerous activity. Climbed a new route? Know about access issues?
[Leave an update](https://www.idahoaclimbingguide.com/submit-book-update/)and help support Idaho’s climbing
community.
