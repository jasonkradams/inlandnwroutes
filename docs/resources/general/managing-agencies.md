## Trailhead Registration & Forest Safety

!!! danger "Emergency Dispatch: Dial 911"

    In any life-threatening emergency, injury, lost hiker situation, or active wildfire report, immediately dial **911**.
    Provide emergency dispatchers with your exact trail name, Forest Service road number, and GPS coordinates if
    available.

!!! tip "Always Register at Trailhead Kiosks"

    **Be sure to log in at every trailhead register box.**

    - **Trail Maintenance Funding:** Visitor log counts directly determine federal and state budget allocations for
      trail repairs, bridge construction, and wilderness preservation.
    - **Search & Rescue Tracking:** In the event of a wilderness emergency or wildfire evacuation, trailhead logs provide
      first responders with critical information regarding missing parties and entry timestamps.
