# Silverstreak Zipline Tours

## About Silver Streak Zipline Tours

Silver Streak Zipline Tours offers a total of 10 ziplines on 250 acres of privately owned, undisturbed,
mountainous terrain. Our most popular course, the West Course, was put up in June of 2012 and we have been
providing fun and excitement ever since.

Enjoy nature at its best! We are conveniently located just off the I-90 at exits 61 and 62. Our office sits
in historic downtown Wallace, ID, just a short drive from our mountain property, where your adventure
begins. Our friendly guides accompany you throughout your tour.
