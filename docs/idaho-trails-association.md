# Idaho Trails Association

## Idaho trails association

As the voice for Idaho’s hikers, Idaho Trails Association promotes the conservation and enjoyment of Idaho’s
backcountry
on foot.
ITA is a nonprofit that works with volunteers, organizations, and government partners to preserve Idaho’s
incredible
trail system through education, maintenance projects, and Wilderness advocacy. Join us today!

[Learn More](https://www.idahotrailsassociation.org)
