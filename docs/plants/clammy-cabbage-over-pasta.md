# Clammy Cabbage Over Pasta

## Clammy cabbage over pasta (2)

---

Ingredients:

Extra Virgin Olive oil 2/3 of a hard of cabbage, sliced 1/2 C chopped onion 1 t minced garlic 2 stocks of
celery with leaves, chopped French style 6 pieces of sun dried tomatoes, marinaded in 1/2 C wine 6 oz of
tomatoes sauce a pinch of chicken base 1 6oz can of clams and juice

Prep

Prepare noddles of your choice.

Cook the cabbage, celery, onion, and garlic in olive oil til near done. Add tomato sauce, sun dried tomatoes
clams and juiced, chicken base to pan, and cook for 1 minute.

In bowls, pour sauce over the noodles.

Enjoy
