# Basil Shrimp Poscuitto Ham Wraps

## Basil shrimp poscuitto ham wraps

---

Ingredients:

Large Basil  leaves
Medium to large shrimp, de-veined and de-shelled.
Poscuitto Ham.      Remove fat sections. Slice thin.
Extra Virgin Olive oil

Crank up the Barbie.     It BBQ Shrimp time!

Take one large shrimp at a time and paint with olive oil (OO).
Wrap in a large basil leaf and paint with OO.
Cut to size Prosciutto ham and wrap the basil-shrimp with it.
Paint with OO.
Wrap another large basil leaf (pained) around it all.
Skewer and BBQ.

BBQ for about 2-3 minutes, on each side.
Be sure to let cool before chomping down on them.
These can be served over rice if desired.

Enjoy
